The organizing idea

Most GTM content is execution advice wearing a framework costume. It decays — channels move, tools change, budgets shift. This wiki separates the layer that doesn't decay from the layer that does:

01 · Foundations — what stays true

GTM strategy, the Channels Bullseye, core frameworks. These are the axioms. Read them once, re-read when confused, quote them when a deal goes sideways.

02 · Playbooks — what executes

Outbound from zero, channel plays, agentic GTM. Concrete motions with steps, owners, and metrics. These change as the market does — that's the point.
