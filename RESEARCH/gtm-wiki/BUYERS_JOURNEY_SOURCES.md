# Buyer's Journey — Vetted Sources (True Gate)

## Verified sources (register these)

### 1. Gartner — Future of Sales research (press release, 2020-09-15)
Primary: https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2020-09-15-gartner-says-80--of-b2b-sales-interactions-between-su
- Buyers typically spend **only 17% of their time** meeting with potential
  suppliers when considering a purchase.
- **33% of all buyers** desire a seller-free sales experience; **44%** for
  millennials.
- Gartner forecasts **80% of B2B sales interactions will occur in digital
  channels by 2025** (forecast, not a measured current stat).

### 2. Gartner Sales Survey (press release, 2025-06-25)
Primary: https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2025-06-25-gartner-sales-survey-finds-61-percent-of-b2b-buyers-prefer-a-rep-free-buying-experience
- **61% of B2B buyers prefer a rep-free buying experience** (2025 survey).

### 3. Forrester — The State of Business Buying, 2024 (2024-12-04)
Primary: https://www.forrester.com/press-newsroom/forrester-the-state-of-business-buying-2024/
- **86% of B2B purchases stall** during the buying process.
- **81% of buyers express dissatisfaction** with their chosen providers.
- **~13 people** on average are involved in the buying decision; **89% of
  purchases involve two or more departments**.
- **~95% of buyers anticipate using genAI** to support their decision/purchase
  process in the next 12 months.

### 4. 6sense — B2B Buyer Experience Report 2025 (~4,000 buyers, global)
Primary: https://6sense.com/science-of-b2b/buyer-experience-report-2025/
- Buying cycle **10.1 months (2025)**, down from **11.3 (2024)**.
- Point of first contact (POFC) moved earlier: **61% of the journey (2025)**
  vs **69% (2024)** — ~6–7 weeks earlier.
- **95% of the time the winning vendor was already on the Day One shortlist**.
- **~4 of 5 deals won by the "pre-contact favorite"** (≈80%).
- Buyers execute **about two-thirds (67%) of the journey before engaging
  sellers**.
- Buying groups are **10+ people**; buyers initiate outreach themselves **>80%
  of the time**.
- Buyers average **8–9 prior purchase journeys** per category.

### 5. CEB (now Gartner) — The Challenger Customer (2015 book; Adamson, Dixon, Toman)
Primary: https://challengerinc.com/the-challenger-customer-profiles/
- Buyer roles: **Mobilizers, Talkers, Blockers**; consensus-based buying.
- **Sellers who target Mobilizers are 31% more likely to be high performers**
  (CEB study of ~5,000 individuals).
- The Challenger Customer study (~5,000 buyers) found the **sales experience**
  was the dimension where buyers most perceived a difference (53% cited sales
  experience — more than brand, service, and value combined).

## Flagged — do NOT register (doubtful / superseded / misattributed)

- **"57% of the journey before vendor contact"** — this is the old 2012 CEB +
  Google stat. It has been superseded by Gartner and 6sense's more recent
  figures (POFC 61%, two-thirds before engaging sellers). Do not cite 57%.
- **"70–80% of the journey completed before sales contact, per Forrester
  (2024)"** — circulated by The Starr Conspiracy but NOT present in Forrester's
  own State of Business Buying 2024 release. Secondary attribution; stays out
  until a primary Forrester source names it.
- **"Buyers spend only 5% of the journey with vendors"** — misattribution /
  conflation. The defensible figures are Gartner 2020's **17% of total buying
  time with ALL suppliers** and the **5–6% with any single vendor** (Gartner,
  cited via 6sense's buying-groups page). The separate "95/5" rule is about
  only 5% of accounts being in-market, not share of journey time. Cite the 17%
  / 5–6% with a named date, not "5%".
- **Salesforce State of Sales / LinkedIn B2B Buying** (optional in the plan) —
  not pulled in this pass; if the page wants a "buyer prefers self-serve /
  seller added value" split, flag it and I will vet it before it ships.
