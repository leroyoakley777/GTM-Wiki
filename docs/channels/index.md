# Channels

The **channels** section covers how to find, evaluate, and execute on your single highest-leverage acquisition channel. It starts with the bullseye framework — how to pick your one best channel instead of spreading thin — then goes deep on each channel.

Every page follows the same rule: **it is a decision, not a default.** Each channel wins under specific conditions (tight ICP, research-driven buyers, self-evident product value, high ACV). Read the bullseye first, then the channel that fits your situation.

## The Framework

- [Channels Bullseye](../foundations/gtm-channels-bullseye) — how to find your one best channel. Start here.

## Channels

- [Outbound](./outbound) — surgical, fastest repeatable pipeline when the ICP is tight. Benchmarks: 3–10 meetings per 1k sends.
- [Inbound](./inbound) — the compounding channel. Benchmarks: AI-referral converts at ~5.8%, organic ~4.9%.
- [Product-Led Growth](./product-led) — the product as salesperson. Benchmarks: ~9% median free-to-paid, ~25–30% with PQLs.
- [Partner / Channel](./partner) — leverage another sales team. Healthy target: 10–20% of new pipeline from partners.
- [Community-Led](./community-led) — the slowest channel, compounds the hardest. Healthy: 30–50% member-initiated referral share.
- [Paid Acquisition](./paid) — the scaler and the eraser. Healthy: CAC payback under 12 months.
- [Events & Field](./events-field) — meet the buyer in person. Healthy: 3–10x pipeline return on event cost.
- [ABM](./abm) — few high-value accounts, 2–3x win-rate lift. Benchmarks: 45% ABM vs 18% non-ABM.
- [Referral & Viral](./referral-viral) — the customer as your sales team. Healthy: viral coefficient >1.0 sustains growth.

## How to use this section

1. **Run the bullseye before you pick a channel.** Most GTM failure is spreading across too many channels, not picking the wrong one.
2. **Match the channel to your economics.** High ACV + tight ICP → outbound or ABM. Research-driven buyers → inbound. Self-evident product value → PLG.
3. **Pair each channel with its foundation and data pages.** Every channel page cross-links to the strategy framework and the benchmark tables — read them together.
