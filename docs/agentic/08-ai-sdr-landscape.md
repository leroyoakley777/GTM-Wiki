# AI SDR Market Map

## Thesis

- Two camps: full-autonomous senders and agent-assisted drafts. The assisted camp keeps a human on the send.
- Vendor claims center on cost per lead and volume. Neither survives contact with deliverability.
- The durable question for any AI SDR: what does it read, and who checks what it writes?

## What breaks in practice

The market sells agents as headcount replacement. Mollick's framing holds better: agents, not chatbots, shift the unit of work to the task, and the task still includes judgment [1]. Buyer-side AI now screens inbound volume, so autonomous volume meets autonomous screening and both sides lose. Evaluate vendors on the anatomy, not the demo: source discipline, draft quality, human gate, and a log of what was sent. EXM7777's machine is a better spec than most vendor pages because every layer is named [2]. Bustamante's recipe test applies to every product in this market: does it own the workflow or just the chat [3]?
