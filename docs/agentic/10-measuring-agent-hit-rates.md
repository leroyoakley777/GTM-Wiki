# Measuring Agent Hit Rates

## Thesis

- An agent's output is a draw from a distribution. Measure the rate, not the anecdote.
- Always measure against a named field baseline. A win rate without a base is decoration.
- Publish the miss rate next to the hit rate. Both go in the run report.

## What breaks in practice

Teams quote the best run. Anthropic's protein-binder agents landed 22 to 35 percent against a field baseline of 10 to 15 percent [1]; the claim works because both numbers are named. Agent claims deserve the same treatment in GTM: sample outputs weekly, grade them on the page contract, and track the trend, not the highlight. Quota hit-rate benchmarks give the human baseline to compare against [2]. A hit rate you cannot reproduce is marketing.
