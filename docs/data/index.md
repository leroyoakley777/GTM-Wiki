# Data

**Benchmarks and reference data** for GTM operators. Conversion rates, SaaS metrics, market segmentation, funnel leakage, pricing, outbound response rates, and sales cycle benchmarks: sourced from real data with named bases, not survey-based vendor decks.

Every page follows the same rule: **baselines, not targets.** Numbers are ranges or medians with a named source. Where sources disagree, the disagreement is shown, because teams define stages differently.

## Benchmarks

- [Conversion Rate Benchmarks](./conversion-rate-benchmarks): stage-by-stage rates across the bowtie, visitor → expansion. The funnel math every GTM plan starts from.
- [GTM Metrics Benchmarks](./gtm-metrics): pipeline coverage, win rate, CAC payback, LTV:CAC, NRR, quota attainment.
- [Market Segmentation Benchmarks](./market-segmentation): the 95-5 rule, four segmentation layers, personalization lifts, TAM/SAM/SOM.
- [Funnel Benchmarks](./funnel): how the funnel leaks stage by stage and why leakage multiplies.
- [Pricing as GTM Benchmarks](./pricing-as-gtm): packaging, discounting, value metrics, and expansion pricing.
- [Outbound Response Rate Benchmarks](./outbound-response-rates): what cold outbound returns, and why multi-touch wins.
- [Sales Cycle Benchmarks](./sales-cycle-benchmarks): how long deals take by deal size and committee size.

## How to use this section

1. **Start with the conversion table** to see the shape of a normal, leaky funnel.
2. **Pair a benchmark with its foundation.** Each data page cross-links to the foundation page that defines the concept. Read them together.
3. **Benchmark against your own trend first.** These tables are the map. Your historical numbers are the territory.
