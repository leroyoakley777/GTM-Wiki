# Frameworks

The **foundations** are the load-bearing walls of this wiki: the concepts every
other section builds on. They are deliberately industry- and stage-agnostic.
Adapt them to any business model.

Read them in order if the section is new to you. Every page here earns its
place by being something an operator can run on Monday morning.

- [What Is Go-to-Market (GTM)?](./what-is-gtm): the definition, the
  vocabulary, and the build sequence.
- [GTM Strategy Framework](./gtm-strategy-framework): the universal revenue
  operating system every other page builds on.
- [GTM Channels Bullseye](./gtm-channels-bullseye): how to find your single
  highest-use acquisition channel.
- [ICP Definition](./icp-definition): how to define, validate, and iterate
  on an Ideal Customer Profile.
- [Positioning and Messaging](./positioning-and-messaging): positioning
  that puts your strengths at the center, then messaging that carries it to
  every buyer touchpoint.
- [Revenue Architecture](./revenue-architecture): the operating system of
  a recurring-revenue business, from the revenue factory to the growth
  formula.
- [Revenue Operations](./revenue-operations): the harness function that
  keeps the model, the pipeline, and the forecast honest.
- [GTM Funnel](./gtm-funnel): the buyer journey from awareness to
  expansion, with measurable stages and conversion benchmarks.
- [GTM Metrics](./gtm-metrics): CAC, LTV, payback, NRR, magic number,
  rule of 40, and how to steer the business with them.
- [Competitive Analysis](./competitive-analysis): who you beat, who beats
  you, and why, at the market level and the deal level.
- [Brand as GTM](./brand-as-gtm): building memory with future buyers and
  turning the 95% who are out of market into the 5% who choose you.
