# 00 — GTM Strategy Framework (Universal)

This is the **foundational framework** that every other section in this wiki builds upon.

## Definition

A **Go-to-Market (GTM) Strategy** is the documented, cross-functional revenue operating system that defines:

- Who you are targeting (ICP)
- Why now and why you win (positioning & value proposition)
- How you reach, convert, and retain them (channel + motion)
- How success is measured and iterated upon

**Key distinction**: Marketing gets attention. GTM turns attention into revenue.

---

## The Six Non-Negotiable Building Blocks

| Building Block              | Core Question                                      | Primary Owner          |
|----------------------------|----------------------------------------------------|------------------------|
| 1. Ideal Customer Profile  | Who is the highest-fit buyer?                      | RevOps + Founders      |
| 2. Value Proposition       | Why do we win for each persona?                    | Marketing + Product    |
| 3. GTM Motion(s)           | How do we acquire and expand?                      | GTM Leadership         |
| 4. Pricing & Packaging     | How does monetization align with value?            | Product + Finance      |
| 5. Channel Strategy        | Where do our buyers live and buy?                  | Marketing + Sales      |
| 6. Measurement & Feedback  | How do we know it’s working and when to pivot?     | RevOps                 |

## Modern GTM Motions (2026)

Most high-performing companies run **2 primary motions**:

- **Product-Led Growth (PLG)**
- **Sales-Led Growth (SLG)**
- **Account-Based Marketing (ABM)**
- **Demand Generation**
- **Content-Led Growth**
- **Community-Led Growth**
- **Partner / Ecosystem-Led**
- **Hybrid** (most common winning pattern)

## 90-Day Implementation Roadmap

### Days 1–30: Foundation
- Interview 15+ recent wins and 5+ losses
- Lock ICP + exclusion criteria
- Choose primary + secondary motion
- Define positioning

### Days 31–60: Build
- Set pricing and packaging
- Stand up 2–3 core channels
- Instrument measurement

### Days 61–90: Launch & Iterate
- Run weekly reviews
- Kill or double-down based on data
