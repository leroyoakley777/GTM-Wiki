# 01 — GTM Channels Bullseye Framework

**Goal**: Efficiently discover your single highest-leverage acquisition channel instead of spreading effort across too many at once.

This is an evolution of the classic *Traction* Bullseye framework, updated for 2025–2026 realities.

---

## Three Rings

### 1. Outer Ring: Brainstorm (Diverge)
Generate **at least one concrete idea** for every major channel category below. Do not judge feasibility yet.

#### 25+ Channel Categories (2026)

**Community & Product**
- Product-Led Growth (PLG)
- Community-Led Growth
- Waitlist / Viral Loops
- Engineering as Marketing
- Viral Marketing / Word of Mouth

**Content & Brand**
- SEO
- Content Marketing & Thought Leadership
- Short-form Video & Audio
- Public Relations (traditional + unconventional)
- Speaking Engagements

**Paid Acquisition**
- Search Engine Marketing (SEM)
- Social & Display Ads
- Programmatic / Intent-based advertising

**Targeted / Outbound**
- Sales (SDR/AE motion)
- Email / Nurture sequences
- Targeted outreach (LinkedIn, X, niche communities)

**Ecosystem & Partnerships**
- Cloud Marketplaces (AWS, GCP, Azure)
- Influencer & Creator programs
- Business Development / Strategic partnerships
- Affiliate / Referral programs
- Platform integrations

**High-Touch / Offline**
- Trade shows & conferences
- Offline events & meetups
- Physical / out-of-home advertising

---

### 2. Middle Ring: Test (Narrow)
Select **3–5 promising channels** and run **cheap, fast, measurable tests**.

**Test Design Rules**:
- Budget/time box each test (e.g., $2k or 2 weeks max)
- Define a clear success metric upfront
- Document learnings even if the test “fails”

---

### 3. Inner Ring: Focus (Bullseye)
Once you have data, **pick one primary channel** and go all-in until it saturates.

This is where the majority of leverage is created.

---

## 2026 Channel Prioritization Heuristics

| Situation                        | Recommended Starting Channels                     | Why |
|----------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------|-----|
| Early-stage / low budget         | Community + Content + PLG                         | Lowest cost, highest learning |
| AI / Developer tools             | Engineering Marketing + Community + Integrations  | Developers trust peers & code |
| High-ACV B2B (> $50k)            | ABM + Targeted Sales + Intent Data                | Precision and relationships matter |
| Prosumer / Creator tools         | Short-form video + Community + Influencers        | Authentic distribution wins |
| Marketplace / Platform           | Ecosystem + Integrations + Partnerships           | Leverage existing user bases |

---

## Common Traps

- Testing too many channels at once
- Scaling before understanding unit economics
- Treating community as a broadcast channel
- Copying what worked for a different business model
