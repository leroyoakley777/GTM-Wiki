# Recruiting

GTM recruiting is selection for a motion you can already describe. If you cannot write the first 30 days of the job on one page, you are not hiring. You are buying hope.

This page covers when to hire, what to select for, how to ramp, and where hiring breaks. Pair it with [SDRs](/docs/roles/sdrs), [AEs](/docs/roles/aes), and [Outbound](/docs/channels/outbound).

## When to make the hire

The first sales hire's critical value is accelerating a company toward product-market fit, not the first dollar they book [262]. If the founder has not sold the product enough times to name the ICP, the objections, and the reason-to-reach, do not hire an SDR to discover those in public.

A practical gate:

- You can name the ICP and the disqualifiers. See [ICP definition](/docs/foundations/icp-definition).
- A founder or founding AE has booked meetings with the current sequence.
- ACV supports a fully-loaded SDR at about $139,120/year [39].
- Someone in the company will inspect the hire's work weekly.

Miss any one of those and the hire becomes an expensive experiment.

## What to select for

Roberge's five traits that correlated with B2B sales success: coachability, curiosity, prior success, intelligence, and work ethic [262]. Hire for what is hard to teach. Integrity, grit, and net-new pipeline generation belong on that list. Deal size and domain can be taught [279].

Score the interview on work sample, not charm.

| Screen | What you collect | Pass looks like |
|--------|------------------|-----------------|
| Written ICP test | 10 accounts they would target from a messy list | They cut as many as they keep |
| Cold email rewrite | Your worst live template | Signal in line one, ask in line last |
| Call review | A recording you give them | They notice the missed question |
| Reference on grit | Prior manager | Specific story of a miss they recovered |

Internally promoted employees perform better and stay longer than external hires in that same research [262]. Look at CS, support, and high-performing SDRs before you pay a premium for a stranger with a polished deck.

## Ramp that is a design, not a vibe

- Week 1: ICP, product, listen to won calls, write reasons-to-reach.
- Weeks 2-3: send a proven sequence with manager edits on Tier 1.
- Month 2: full desk, still inspected.
- Month 3: judge held meetings and accepted opportunities.

Attrition bands from 20VC: scaling companies should target about 25% annual sales attrition. 35% is too high. 15% is often too low because the bottom is not being moved [279]. Recruiting that never exits looks kind and lowers the bar.

## How recruiting differs by stage

- **Seed.** Founder hires one generalist who can prospect and close. Do not build an SDR org.
- **Series A desk.** First specialized SDRs only after the sequence is proven. See [Outbound](/docs/channels/outbound).
- **Scale.** A hiring formula beats a heroic recruiter [262]. Same screen, same ramp, same scorecard. Verticalize later than you think. Databricks waited past $500M ARR to verticalize [279].

## Failure modes

- **Hiring closers and calling them prospectors.** The funnel collapses at the top. See [SDRs](/docs/roles/sdrs).
- **Quota as personality test.** Over-aggressive quotas drive out A-players [279].
- **Ignoring the sample.** Charm in the interview, empty calendar in month two.
- **No manager capacity.** A hire without an inspector is a skipped ramp.
- **Buying experience in the wrong motion.** An enterprise hunter dropped into a $8k ACV outbound desk will prospect like the last job, not this one.

## Agentic layer

Agents help screen written work (email rewrites, ICP tests) against a rubric. Humans still make the hire, run the reference, and own the first 90 days. Do not let a model grade "hunger." Grade the artifact.
