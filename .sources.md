## GTM Frameworks (foundations)

1. **nRev — GTM Frameworks: 8 Go-to-Market Models**
   - URL: https://www.nrev.ai/blog/gtm-frameworks
   - Published: Jun 8, 2026
   - Covers: Bullseye, Crossing the Chasm, GTM Motions, Bowtie, AARRR, Flywheel, T2D3, Rule of 40
   - Good for: overview of named frameworks, when to use each

2. **Traction — Gabriel Weinberg & Justin Mares (book)**
   - Origin of the Bullseye Framework
   - 19 traction channels, test-then-concentrate

3. **Crossing the Chasm — Geoffrey Moore (book)**
   - Origin of beachhead strategy, technology adoption curve
   - Innovators → Early Adopters → Early Majority → Late Majority → Laggards

4. **Winning by Design — Jacco van der Kooij (Bowtie framework)**
   - Origin of the Bowtie model (recurring revenue journey)

5. **HubSpot — Flywheel**
   - Origin: Brian Halligan, borrowing from Jim Collins' Good to Great
   - Attract → Engage → Delight

6. **Dave McClure — AARRR (Pirate Metrics)**
   - Origin: 500 Startups (500 Global)
   - Acquisition → Activation → Retention → Referral → Revenue

7. **OpenView / Blake Bartlett — Product-Led Growth**
   - Origin of PLG as a named motion

8. **Neeraj Agrawal (Battery Ventures) — T2D3**
   - Triple, Triple, Double, Double, Double benchmark
